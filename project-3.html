Soloprenual

As an entrepreneuer, the objective was to create a product line that was both visually engaging and commercially viable. Stickers were chosen for their cost-effectiveness and accessibility to many. They can easily serve as personal embellishments and self-expression tools on various surfaces, such as laptops, eater bottles and journals. The imaginative use of animals and food adds a unique blend of charm and cleverness, differentiating the brand from conventional sticker products.

This project explores the interpretation of animals and food through a series of sticker designs. By merging animals with culinary art, it interprets the familiar into something imaginative and unexpected. The concept of the stickers invites people to see how the ordinary can be reimagined by reshaping familiar form.
