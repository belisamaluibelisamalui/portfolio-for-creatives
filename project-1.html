JAMZ

JAMZ is a small batch jam brand that celebrates the changing seasons of British Columbia through unique blends of locally harvested fruits, vegetables and herbs. Each flavour is thoughtfully curated to capture the characteristics of the season by combining local fruits, vegetables and herbs in unexpected combinations. More than just a line of jams, every jar offers a taste of the BC landscape and an insight of the process of growth, harvesting and preservation. Each jar transforms these seasonal ingredients into a quirky flavour experience that celebrates the character of its season.
