  • Hello!

    My name is Bel, and I am a graphic designer with a vivid imagination that transforms unlikely ideas into delightful little wonders. I believe design is not just about visuals but about creating experiences that spark curiosity, emotion, and connection.

    I am deeply inspired by the natural world and the quiet poetry found in everyday life, such as a shadow on the wall, the hum of a late afternoon, or the way light softens a color just before sunset. These fleeting details often find their way into my work, shaping designs that feel alive, human, and full of character.

    Curiosity drives everything I do. I am always exploring new techniques, materials, and ideas through illustration, branding, and storytelling. I love finding beauty in unexpected places and turning simple concepts into something meaningful and memorable. My process is both intuitive and intentional. I listen closely, observe carefully, and craft with care

    At the heart of my practice is a desire to connect and to make people feel something. Whether it is a brand that tells a heartfelt story, a playful label that makes someone smile, or a design that invites a moment of wonder, my goal is to create work that lingers and feels like a small piece of magic in an ordinary day.

    I am constantly learning, refining, and pushing the limits of my creativity. Every project is a new adventure and a chance to tell a story in a way that feels honest, imaginative, and beautifully human.
