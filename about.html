  • Hello!

    I'm Bel, a graphic designer driven by curiosity, imagination, and a love for storytelling. I work across branding, illustration, typography, and visual design, creating thoughtful experiences that spark curiosity, emotion, and connection.

    Inspired by the natural world and the quiet poetry of everyday life, I find beauty in unexpected places and turn simple ideas into meaningful visual stories. My approach blends intuition with intention, exploring playful concepts, distinctive details, and unexpected possibilities.

    At the heart of my work is a desire to make people feel something. Whether through a thoughtful brand identity, a playful illustration, or an experimental typeface, I strive to create designs that feel human, memorable, and full of character.
