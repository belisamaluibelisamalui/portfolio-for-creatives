Ink Blot

Type Design

Experimental Typeface

Ink Blot is inspired by the natural movement of ink and a dip pen. Organic curves, irregular edges and variations in stroke weight give each character a quality of handwritten letterforms. The typeface explores the balance between control and imperfection, all the while intending to evoke a quiet, reflective ambience. It represents the instant where ink touches paper for the first time and the hesitation of human hand movements before the motion starts.
