Mend is an educational and studio program that offers workshops in woodworking and metalworking, enabling women, transgender, and non-binary individuals who have been formerly incarcerated to learn these crafts in an accessible setting. The purpose of the project is to establish a safe and inclusive environment where individuals can gain confidence, learn practical arts, and express themselves through the process of making something by hand.
