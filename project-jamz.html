JAMZ is a small batch jam brand that celebrates the changing seasons of British Columbia through unique blends of locally harvested fruits, vegetables and herbs. Each flavour is thoughtfully curated to capture the characteristics of the season by combining local fruits, vegetables and herbs in unexpected combinations.

More than just a line of jams, every jar offers a taste of the BC landscape and an insight of the process of growth, harvesting and preservation. Each jar transforms these seasonal ingredients into a quirky flavour experience that celebrates the character of its season.

Rhubarb + Rosemary

A Bright, Herbal Twist on a BC Classic

Tart, vibrant rhubarb meets the earthy, aromatic notes of rosemary in an unexpected seasonal pairing. This small-batch jam balances bright fruitiness with a savoury herbal finish.

Peach + Lemon Balm

Summer Peach with a Fresh Herbal Lift

Sweet, sun-ripened peach meets the delicate citrus notes of lemon balm for a fresh, fragrant combination. A playful take on summer preserves that captures the lighter side of seasonal BC flavours.

Cranberry + Pear and Sage

A Cosy Seasonal Blend with a Savoury Edge

Bright, tart cranberries are softened by mellow pear, while sage adds an earthy, aromatic depth. This distinctive small-batch blend brings a savoury twist to the comforting flavours of the cooler season.
